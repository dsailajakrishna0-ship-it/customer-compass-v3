Elena Torres (Owner, Harbor & Pine) joined the quarterly check-in call. She
said she is happy with support response times since switching to the Retail
support package earlier this year, and specifically called out that the team
resolved a point-of-sale sync issue within a few hours during a busy weekend.

Elena confirmed she plans to renew the Retail support package when it comes
up for renewal and does not need any changes to her current plan. She asked
one question: whether the support package includes coverage during major
holidays, since Harbor & Pine is a retail business and holidays are their
busiest and highest-risk period.

We told her holiday coverage is included and confirmed the on-call schedule
for the upcoming holiday season would be shared with her by email.
