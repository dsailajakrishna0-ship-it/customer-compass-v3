Dr. Sarah Kim (Clinic Director at Riverside Health Group) called to confirm
renewal of the Premium Operations Plan and to discuss expanding coverage to
a second clinic location opening next quarter.

We confirmed the renewal and explained that adding a second location under
the same plan only requires a site-license add-on rather than a brand-new
contract. Dr. Kim asked whether staff scheduling data from the new location
could sync automatically with the existing dashboard from day one; we
confirmed it can, as long as the new location is provisioned at least two
weeks before go-live.
