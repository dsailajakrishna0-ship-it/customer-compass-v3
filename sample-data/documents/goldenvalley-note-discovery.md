Carlos Mendez (Operations Manager at Golden Valley Foods) joined a discovery
call to evaluate Fleet Visibility for tracking shipment delays across
several third-party co-packers. He explained that right now his team finds
out about a delay only when a retail customer calls to complain, which is
too late to do anything about it.

We walked through how Fleet Visibility consolidates delivery status across
multiple carriers into one dashboard and can alert operations staff as soon
as a shipment crosses a delay threshold. Carlos asked whether the
integration would require each co-packer to adopt new software on their
side; we confirmed it only requires read access to each carrier's existing
tracking feed, no co-packer-side changes needed. He said this removes his
biggest concern about rollout complexity.
