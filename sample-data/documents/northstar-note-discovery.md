Attendees: Jordan Brooks (Head of Customer Success, Northstar Logistics)

Northstar is evaluating our platform to give their customer success team
real-time visibility into delivery status for fleet customers. Today their
reps manually check three separate carrier portals and compile a summary by
hand each morning, which Jordan estimated takes about two hours per day
across the team.

Jordan's top priorities, in order:
1. A single view that shows delivery status per fleet customer.
2. Alerts when a delivery is delayed beyond a configurable threshold.
3. The ability to share a read-only status link with the fleet customer
   directly, to reduce inbound "where is my shipment" calls.

Jordan asked for a proposal covering the Fleet Visibility rollout, including
implementation timeline and whether alerting is included in the base package
or sold as an add-on.
