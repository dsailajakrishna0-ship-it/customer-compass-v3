Customer: James Okafor (IT Director at Riverside Health Group)

James asked how patient-adjacent operational data (appointment scheduling
metadata, not clinical records) is stored and whether retention settings
meet their internal compliance requirements. He noted that Riverside's
compliance team requires configurable data-retention windows and audit
logs for any system touching scheduling data.

We confirmed that the platform supports configurable retention windows per
account and that an audit log of data-access events is available on
request through support. James said he would forward this to Riverside's
compliance team and asked for written confirmation he could share
internally; we agreed to send a summary document.
