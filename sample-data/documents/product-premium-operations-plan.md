The Premium Operations Plan is designed for manufacturing and industrial
customers running one or more physical sites.

Included in the base price for a single site:
- Full operations dashboard, including interaction history and export tools.
- Up to three implementation/onboarding sessions per year.
- Standard support with a next-business-day response target.

Multi-site pricing:
- Each additional site is billed at 70% of the single-site rate.
- Accounts with three or more sites receive an automatic 10% discount on the
  combined multi-site total.
- One implementation session can cover multiple sites if they go live within
  the same 30-day window; otherwise, each site's go-live counts toward the
  three included sessions per year.

Known limitation (as of this quarter): exporting more than 90 days of
interaction history in one request can time out. The recommended workaround
is exporting in 30-day windows. A background export feature is in progress.
