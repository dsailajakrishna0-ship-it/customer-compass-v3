Dana Whitfield (then Project Manager at Summit Builders) joined an exit
interview call after Summit's Site Ops Suite deal did not close and their
account was marked as a former customer. Dana explained that Summit had
frozen all new vendor contracts company-wide due to a budget restructuring,
and that this was unrelated to any dissatisfaction with the product itself.

Dana said the team had found the evaluation period useful and would
consider revisiting Site Ops Suite once the budget freeze lifts, likely in
a future fiscal year. She asked to stay on the mailing list for product
updates in the meantime.
