Priya Nandakumar (VP Engineering at Vertex Analytics) called ahead of their
annual renewal to ask about upgrading from the standard plan to Analytics
Pro. She said her team wants custom dashboards for tracking deployment
frequency and incident response time, which the standard plan doesn't
support.

We confirmed Analytics Pro includes unlimited custom dashboards and a
dedicated API quota. Priya asked whether the upgrade could be billed
mid-cycle rather than waiting for the renewal date, and we agreed to send a
prorated quote.
