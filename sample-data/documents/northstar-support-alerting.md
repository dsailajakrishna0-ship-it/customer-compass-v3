Customer: Jordan Brooks (evaluating Fleet Visibility rollout, not yet a
customer)

Jordan asked, ahead of signing, whether delay alerts can be configured per
fleet customer or only globally for the whole account. He said Northstar's
fleet customers have very different tolerance for what counts as "delayed" —
a same-day courier customer cares about 30-minute delays, while a long-haul
freight customer only cares about delays over 4 hours.

We confirmed that alert thresholds are configurable per customer, not just
globally, and that this is included in the base Fleet Visibility package with
no add-on required. Jordan said this addresses his main concern and he plans
to bring a rollout proposal to his leadership team next week.
