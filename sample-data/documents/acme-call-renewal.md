Maya Chen (Operations Director at Acme Fabrication) called to discuss renewing
the Premium Operations Plan ahead of the October contract date. She asked
whether the premium tier includes onboarding support for two additional
factory floors that Acme is bringing online next quarter.

We explained that the Premium Operations Plan includes up to three
implementation sessions per year at no extra cost, and that additional
sessions are billed at the standard consulting rate. Maya said budget approval
depends on knowing the total cost before she can commit to the renewal, so we
agreed to send a written pricing breakdown.

Maya also mentioned that the dashboard export feature has been slow for her
team recently, and asked whether that was a known issue.
