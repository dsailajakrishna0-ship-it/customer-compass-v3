---
title: Support ticket, uneven coverage after sprayer calibration
type: support
date: 2026-04-11
---

A customer reported streaky coverage on a 40 hectare apple block after
switching to the new low-drift nozzles. Support asked for the ground speed, the
nozzle codes and the pressure used.

Cause: the sprayer had been calibrated for the old nozzles at 3 bar. The
low-drift nozzles need 4.5 bar to reach the label flow rate, so the outer rows
received roughly 30 percent less product.

Resolution: recalibrate at 4.5 bar, re-treat the under-dosed rows within the
re-entry window, and record the new settings on the block board. No product
credit was issued because the label rate was respected on the second pass.
