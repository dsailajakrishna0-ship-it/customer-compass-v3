---
title: Cold chain handling for seedlings and biologicals
type: product
date: 2026-03-09
---

Biological products (beneficial insects, bacterial inoculants) and bare-root
seedlings must stay between 2 and 6 degrees Celsius from the warehouse to the
customer door.

Dispatch rules:
- Load refrigerated pallets last and unload them first.
- A data logger travels in every cold pallet; a reading above 8 degrees
  Celsius for more than 30 minutes voids the shipment.
- Never stack seedling trays more than four high: the bottom tray loses
  viability within two days.

If a customer reports a warm delivery, photograph the logger display, quarantine
the remaining stock from that pallet and open a replacement order the same day.
