---
title: Product recall procedure
type: support
date: 2026-07-01
---

A recall starts when a supplier or a regulator flags a lot. The quality manager
opens a recall record within one hour and freezes the lot in the warehouse
system.

Within four hours, support lists every customer who received the lot, using the
lot number on the delivery notes. Customers are contacted by phone first, then
confirmed by email, and told to stop using the product and to keep the remaining
stock aside.

Collected stock is returned on the next delivery. Credits are issued only against
returned or documented stock. Close the record once every customer has been
reached and the regulator has been notified.
