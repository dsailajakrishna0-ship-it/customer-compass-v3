---
title: Crop protection product handbook, re-entry and harvest intervals
type: product
date: 2026-03-02
---

Every Orchard Valley crop protection product carries two intervals on its label,
and field crews must know both before any treated block is entered.

The re-entry interval (REI) is the time after spraying before workers may enter
the block without protective equipment. Most fungicides in the Valley range have
a 12 hour REI; the copper-based products have 24 hours; the concentrated
insecticide OV-Insect-40 has 48 hours.

The pre-harvest interval (PHI) is the minimum number of days between the last
application and harvest. For stone fruit the PHI of OV-Insect-40 is 14 days; for
leafy greens it is 7 days. A block sprayed inside its PHI must not be picked, and
the packhouse will reject the lot at intake.

Record the product, the rate, the time sprayed and the REI end time on the block
board before leaving the field.
