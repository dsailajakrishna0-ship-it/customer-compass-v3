---
title: Seed lot shelf life and germination testing
type: product
date: 2026-03-16
---

Each seed lot is tested for germination before release and again at nine
months. A lot may be sold while germination stays above 85 percent for vegetable
seed and above 90 percent for cereal seed.

Shelf life depends on storage: sealed foil packs held below 15 degrees Celsius
and 40 percent humidity keep for up to three years. Opened packs should be used
within one season.

The lot number printed on the pack is the only way to trace a complaint. Ask
every customer for the lot number before agreeing to a credit or a replacement.
