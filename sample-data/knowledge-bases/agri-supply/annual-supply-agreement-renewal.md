---
title: Email on the annual supply agreement renewal for Stonebridge Orchards
type: email
date: 2026-06-14
---

Stonebridge Orchards asked whether the annual supply agreement renews at the
same pricing. Our reply: the volume discount tiers stay as they are, 5 percent
above 20 tonnes and 8 percent above 40 tonnes of fertiliser, but fuel and
freight surcharges follow the quarterly index.

Renewal is automatic unless either party gives notice 60 days before the end of
the agreement year. Prices for seed and biologicals are fixed for the season
once the order is confirmed.

Stonebridge also asked about early payment: a 2 percent discount applies when an
invoice is paid within 10 days.
