---
title: Note on organic certification for the Willow Creek co-op
type: note
date: 2026-05-05
---

The Willow Creek co-op asked which products in the Valley range are allowed
under their organic certification. Only the copper fungicide, the sulphur
products and the biological controls are listed by the certifier; every other
crop protection product is excluded.

The certifier requires the co-op to keep purchase invoices and block spray
records for five years. We agreed to send a yearly statement of purchases each
January so the co-op does not need to search its own records.

Follow-up: confirm in writing that OV-Insect-40 is not organic, since a member
had planned to use it near the packhouse.
