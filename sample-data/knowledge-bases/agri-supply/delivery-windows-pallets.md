---
title: Delivery windows and pallet rules
type: product
date: 2026-04-20
---

Standard orders ship on Tuesdays and Fridays. Cold chain orders ship Monday to
Thursday only, so that a delivery never waits at a depot over a weekend.

Pallets are 1.2 by 1.0 metres and carry at most 900 kilograms. A mixed pallet
may combine dry and chemical products only when the chemicals are sealed and
placed below nothing else; fertiliser and seed are never shipped on one pallet.

Orders placed before 14:00 are picked the same day. A missed delivery window is
rebooked at no charge once; the second rebooking is billed as a handling fee.
